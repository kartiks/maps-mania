      Sources & Honesty Box

      Maps lie when data is vague. Ours show their receipts.

      World Bank — World Development IndicatorsPopulation, density, growth, fertility, birth rate, life expectancy, GDP, unemployment, literacy, internet, urban, forest, rainfall, CO₂, renewables & more (CC-BY 4.0). Stored as a local record in data/wb/ (built from the official WDI bulk download; refresh with scripts/fetch-data.mjs). The page re-checks the World Bank API in the background and updates itself if newer data exists — it never waits on the live API to draw the map.
      Consumption, language & climate snapshotsCoffee (ICO), tea (FAOSTAT/industry), beer (Kirin Beer University), wine (OIV), living languages (Ethnologue 2024), mean temperature (World Bank CCKP 1991–2020 normals), all curated in data/curated-extra.js via scripts/build-curated-extra.py — rounded, approximate, for discovery not engineering.
      Biodiversity, volcanoes & political mapsBird/amphibian/reptile richness (IUCN Red List), Holocene volcanoes (Smithsonian GVP), time zones (IANA), driving side (WorldStandards) and landlocked status incl. doubly-landlocked Liechtenstein & Uzbekistan (UN-OHRLLS) — curated, rounded, same honesty bar as above.
      Dot layers — eruptions, cities, airports, meteorites, quakesIconic eruptions (Smithsonian GVP, VEI), ~40 megacities (UN World Urbanization Prospects), ~40 busiest airports (ACI 2023), famous meteorite falls (Meteoritical Society) — curated in data/points.js via scripts/build-points.py. Earthquakes: every M6.0+ since 2000 (~4,000 events) from the USGS ANSS Catalog, saved as data/earthquakes.json (built with scripts/build-earthquakes.py) with live background refresh. Public domain where stated.
      Awards, heritage & blocsNobel laureates (nobelprize.org), all-time Olympic medals (Olympedia; per-capita computed with UN population), UNESCO World Heritage Sites, Freedom House democracy status, legal traditions (JuriGlobe), plug types & metric (WorldStandards), EU/NATO/BRICS/OPEC/G20 membership, dominant language families (Ethnologue), government type (CIA Factbook), coffee production (FAO), chocolate consumption (industry reports) — curated in data/curated-extra.js, rounded and approximate.
      Map boundaries — India shown as per Government of IndiaWorld boundaries vendored locally in data/world.geo.json (Natural Earth via johan/world.geo.json), with India's outline replaced by the official Survey-of-India-aligned boundary — full Jammu & Kashmir (incl. PoK/Gilgit-Baltistan), Ladakh (incl. Aksai Chin) and Arunachal Pradesh — dissolved from data/india-states-official.geojson (MIT; india-in-data/kashmir + state data). Rebuild: scripts/build-india-world.py. The ocean is a fixed colour and is never recoloured by data.
      UN Population Division (WPP 2024)Median age & demographic estimates. UN DESA, World Population Prospects 2024.
      UNESCO Institute for StatisticsAdult literacy (via World Bank SE.ADT.LITR.ZS). Note: literacy is not measured every year — gaps are real, not bugs.
      IAEA PRIS (2024)Operational nuclear reactors by country. Power Reactor Information System.
      CIA World FactbookHighest peaks, coastline length. Public domain.
      HydroLAKES / IUCN / Paleobiology DBLake counts (HydroLAKES v1.0), mammal richness (IUCN Red List), dinosaur fossil localities (Paleobiology Database) — curated snapshots, rounded, for discovery not engineering.
      Commonwealth Secretariat56 member nations (2024, incl. Gabon & Togo).
      ⚠️ Read this: “No data” (hatched grey) means the source has no value for that country-year — we never invent numbers. Curated datasets are single-year snapshots, so the timeline is hidden for them. Aggregates like “World” / “EU” are excluded from the record and rankings. Antarctica is shown but carries no data in any series. India is depicted as per the official map of the Government of India / Survey of India.
